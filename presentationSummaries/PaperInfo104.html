AI-Generated Music on YouTube: Predicting High Viewership and Tracking Audience Reactions Over Time
October 24, 2026: 10:30 AM - 11:00 AM
AI & Emerging Technologies, Conf. Ctr., 205

Authors Abstract
Hunter Willard, Patrick Pashna ABSTRACT&nbsp;This study examines AI-generated music on YouTube from two connected perspectives: what characteristics are&nbsp;associated with high viewership and how audience reactions to AI-generated music change over time. For the first&nbsp;research question, 3,967 AI-generated music videos were analyzed using logistic regression, decision tree, random&nbsp;forest, and XGBoost models, followed by feature-importance and genre analyses. XGBoost provided the strongest&nbsp;overall predictive performance, and the results showed that content-related characteristics, especially genre, artistmimicry or cover references, and AI-vocal references, were more important for predicting high viewership than basic&nbsp;metadata such as hashtags, description length, or mentions of specific AI platforms. For the second research question,&nbsp;46,443 multilingual YouTube comments from 2,344 videos were analyzed using LLM-assisted theme discovery,&nbsp;translation, sentiment coding, and quarterly trend analysis. The results show that audience discussion gradually moved&nbsp;away from early fascination with realism and voice recreation toward greater attention to artist impact, ethics, consent,&nbsp;credit, and limitations of AI-generated music, while overall positive sentiment also declined over time. Taken together,&nbsp;the findings suggest that AI-generated music is moving beyond a stage of technological novelty. As technology&nbsp;becomes more common, success appears to depend more on the type and positioning of the music itself, while&nbsp;audiences increasingly judge AI-generated content based on its quality, fairness, and effects on human artists. The&nbsp;study therefore provides an integrated view of both the platform performance and changing audience reception of AIgenerated music. &nbsp;Keywords: AI-generated music; YouTube; music popularity; audience reception; machine learning; XGBoost;&nbsp;sentiment analysis; thematic analysis; generative AI; social media analytics &nbsp;
