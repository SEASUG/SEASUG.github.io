Viewing and Editing SAS Data in SAS Visual Analytics and SAS Data and AI Studio
October 23, 2026: 11:00 AM - 11:30 AM
Visualization & Reporting, Conf. Ctr., 209

Authors Abstract
Scott Leslie Effective report development inSAS Visual AnalyticsandSAS Data and AI Studiobegins with a clear understanding of the underlying data. The enhancedData Viewerdelivers a fast, intuitive, and highly interactive environment for exploring, validating, and preparing data&mdash;whether working with small tables or massive, enterprise‑scale datasets. Attendees will learn how to efficiently inspect source data, apply filters and sorting, search for values, and make targeted updates through built‑in editing capabilities such as modifying cells, adding or removing rows and columns, and integrating content directly from external sources like Excel.This session will demonstrate how the Data Viewer streamlines common data‑review tasks across SAS Viya applications, enabling users to resolve discrepancies, accelerate report preparation, and maintain data quality with minimal friction. Participants will also get a preview of upcoming enhancements, including audit tracking, undo/redo functionality, and emergingAI‑assisted editing featuresdesigned to anticipate user actions and further optimize workflow. Join us to see how SAS is redefining web‑based data interaction with a modern, desktop‑like experience that transforms the way you explore and refine your data.
